# Daily plan: 21 days, about 4.5 hours a day

The goal of each day is not to read more. It is to **say** a few answers well.
About 3 new modules a day, plus revision of old ones, plus speaking aloud.

---

## One day (about 4.5 hours)

| Time | Block | What to do |
|------|-------|-----------|
| 30 min | **1. Warm-up revision** | Take yesterday's modules. Look only at the keywords. Say each answer aloud. Then check the text and note what you missed. |
| 45 min | **2. Understand new modules** | Read "In simple words" and "The flow". If something is unclear, open the long module (01–08) for that topic only. |
| 60 min | **3. Memorize** | For each new "Say this": read it aloud 3 times → cover it → write the keywords on paper → say it from the keywords → check. Repeat until you can say it twice without looking. |
| 60 min | **4. Speaking practice** | Record yourself on your phone answering the "Same answer, different questions" list. Listen once. Fix **one** thing (a missing step, a weak start, speaking too fast). Record again. |
| 30 min | **5. Spaced revision** | Say the modules from 3 days ago and 7 days ago (see the rule below). |
| 15–30 min | **6. Story + mini mock** | Say one project story from [00-my-project-stories.md](00-my-project-stories.md). Then answer one random practice question from any finished module. |

**Spaced revision rule**: say each module again on **day +1, day +3 and day +7** after you learn it. That is how it moves into long-term memory.

## Speaking techniques that work

- **Keywords first.** Before speaking, say the keyword chain silently. Then turn each keyword into one sentence.
- **60-second answers.** Most answers should fit in 60–90 seconds. Use a timer.
- **Draw while speaking.** For flow modules, draw the boxes on paper while you say the diagram script. Interviewers like it, and it guides your memory.
- **Explain to a friend.** Once a week, explain three modules to someone non-technical. If they understand, your English is simple enough.
- **Record and compare.** Keep your day-1 and day-21 recordings. You will hear the progress.
- **Don't memorize word for word.** Memorize the order and the keywords. Small changes in wording are fine and sound more natural.
