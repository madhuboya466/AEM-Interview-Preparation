## How every module is built

1. **In simple words**: the idea in two or three lines.
2. **Say this**: the answer to memorize (4–7 sentences).
3. **The flow, step by step**: what happens first, next and last, when there is a sequence or diagram.
4. **Diagram script**: what to say while pointing at a diagram or drawing it on a whiteboard.
5. **My example**: from your own project, labelled **[Hands-on]**. When you have no project example, it says **[Theory]** and gives you an honest sentence to use instead.
6. **Keywords**: 5–7 words. If you remember them in order, you can rebuild the whole answer.
7. **Same answer, different questions**: how the interviewer might ask it, and which sentence to start with.
8. **Practice**: two or three questions to answer aloud.

## The answer pattern (use it for every technical question)

You do not need to build every sentence from nothing. Fill these five slots:

| Slot | What you say | Starter phrase |
|------|-------------|----------------|
| **1. What it is** | One-line definition | "X is…" / "X is used to…" |
| **2. How it works** | The steps, in order (3–5) | "It works in a few steps. First… then… finally…" |
| **3. Why it matters** | One line on purpose or risk | "This is important because…" |
| **4. My example** | One project story (from the stories file) | "In my project, …" |
| **5. Close** | One line on what you check or prevent | "So when I see this issue, I first check…" |

If the question is short, use slots 1, 2 and 4 only. If the interviewer wants more, continue with 3 and 5.

### When it is theory (not your hands-on work)

Be honest and still confident:
> "I haven't worked on this directly in my project, but I understand how it works. [Slot 1 and 2.] In my project the closest thing I did was [related story]."

### When you forget the next sentence

Look at your keyword chain in your head and say the next keyword as a sentence. A short pause is fine. Do not restart the answer.

### Safe phrases that buy you thinking time

- "Let me explain it step by step."
- "There are two parts to this."
- "I'll start from where the request comes in."
- "Let me give you an example from my project."
